The goal is to reproduce the paper's published system performance before moving to reinforcement learning. The three targets are deficiency of power supply probability, energy waste possibility, and levelized cost of energy. Ten designs give thirty target values. The completed independent run contains 2,557 complete settings. These are benchmark-fitting results, not held-out validation or a successful paper replication. The simulation results were completed on 31 October 2026; this presentation is prepared on 9 November 2026.

Three comparison runs use two code implementations: the teacher file and the independent paper-first code. Do not confuse these with the three operating modes. The teacher implementation is preserved and executed as supplied. The independent reference counts rejected charging heat, recalculates shortage after actual discharge limits, enforces hydrogen resources, and sizes hydrogen storage by the maximum daily mass. The grid-selected candidate changes shared assumptions in that same independent model, not the published row capacities. The 40-year horizon and balance-of-plant sizing proxy are unverified; the proxy may double-count reactor capital costs. Mode 3 uses the published threshold at the start of the hour. Historical experiments included unchanged-input, adjusted-input, and digitized-input studies. The earlier unchanged-input result had 281.09% RMS error; the adjusted-input 45.66% and digitized-input 71.02% results are not eligible under the current restriction.

The paper has three Mode 1 rows, three Mode 2 rows and four Mode 3 rows, not eleven rows. The additional Mode 3d solution also satisfies a cost cap of approximately 0.0940 dollars per kWh. We copy each design exactly, including its PV count, TES capacity, SOEC rating, demand factor and TES threshold. DF is the demand allocation fraction; the threshold is a fraction of stored heat, not an efficiency. Each row starts independently at 5% of its own TES capacity. The Excel weather and load records are fixed arrays, not constant values over the year. Ambient temperature affects PV output; pressure and tank geometry are not explicit inputs to this reduced model. Historical data are sufficient, but identical author data have not been established.

The code enumerates the physical grid using Cartesian products. Five charge rates times five discharge rates times four rating interpretations times two PV conventions times two reactor ratings times two inactive-SOEC policies times two switching interpretations gives 1,600 physical settings. Every physical case runs all ten designs and is audited. Rank physical agreement using the worst DPSP/EWP relative error plus 0.1 times RMS relative error. Retain four distinct annual-summary signatures, not full-hourly trajectory hashes. For those, evaluate 240 financial combinations, reusing physical results because finance does not change dispatch. Four default finance combinations were already saved, giving 956 additional configurations. The additive hydrogen equation is one separate resource-invalid diagnostic. This staged search is not an exhaustive search of all 384,000 physical-financial pairs.

The authors optimize equipment sizes to find solutions. Our current task instead evaluates their published solutions with an independently implemented forward model. The target values are never substituted into PV, TES or hydrogen equations. Reset TES, run all 8,760 records and compute annual unmet energy, waste and net cost. A 5% reproduction tolerance is relative to each published number, not five percentage points. Row 1a illustrates why a good LCOE does not establish reproduction. A lower DPSP would mean better reliability within the simulated model, but still fails numerical reproduction when the paper reports more unmet demand. Energy conservation is necessary, but does not identify the authors' exact model or hourly data.

With constant reactor production, no hydrogen in Mode 1, the supplied inputs and lossless TES, annual generation minus demand minus the maximum additional stored heat implies an optimistic EWP lower bound. For row 1a it is 8.4323% with the 150 W interpretation, compared with the published 4.02% and the 10% relative-error upper limit of 4.422%. The area-based PV interpretation makes the bound higher. This is conditional evidence of input/model incompatibility, not proof that the paper is wrong. Other unresolved choices include main-text 25 versus appendix 50 MWth and area-based 189.44 W versus appendix 150 W PV. The teacher code also clips charging before waste accounting and can calculate shortage before applying actual discharge limits; the independent model fixes these and audits resources. Its largest checked hourly residual is about 2.24e-13 in the respective energy units. Eighty hourly NPZs and an exact frozen rerun were verified. The 2024 battery/TES model is a separate cross-check: its constant 20 MWe exceeds every demand in the supplied Excel, so positive paper DPSP cannot be reproduced without a different input/model interpretation. No 2024 optimization-algorithm replication was performed. Request clarification rather than silently changing inputs or starting RL.
